Tranzoop Vision · Physical Business Intelligence

Turn cameras, access points and sensors into evidence — not footage.

Tranzoop Vision doesn't operate as a standalone CCTV analytics product. It connects CCTV, access control, QR/NFC/RFID, workforce, machines, IoT, utilities, vehicles and inventory into one loop: Detect → Correlate → Audit → Quantify → Recommend → Act.

Privacy-by-design vision modes
Mode 1
Anonymous Vision

People / vehicle / object detection — no identity involved.

Mode 2
Operational Correlation

Vision + zones + work orders + access events.

Mode 3
Identity-Assisted

Authorised identity signals — app, QR, NFC, card — where necessary.

Mode 4
Biometric Identification

Optional facial recognition, only where justified, secured and legally appropriate.
